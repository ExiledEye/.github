# Code of Conduct

I want to keep this community respectful, clean, and focused on development. 

## Expected Guidelines
* **Be Respectful:** Treat other users and contributors with basic decency. Disagreements are fine, but keep them polite and constructive.
* **Stay on Topic:** Keep comments, issues, and pull requests focused strictly on the project, bug fixes, or feature improvements.

## Enforcement
As the maintainer, I reserve the right to edit, close, or delete comments and issues that violate these guidelines, and block users who cause persistent issues.
